StockPulse Guide

Explore the StockPulse app

The landing page gives you the market snapshot. After login, StockPulse provides a broader set of portfolio, research and analysis tools.

🏠 Dashboard

Your starting point after login. Review portfolio value, performance, DSEX information, charts and market-related signals from one dashboard.

💼 Portfolio & Trading

➕ Buy / ➖ Sell

Record buy and sell transactions with portfolio, quantity, price and date information.

💼 Holdings

View your recorded holdings and portfolio positions in one place.

📜 Trade History

Review historical buy and sell activity and related transaction summaries.

📂 Multiple Portfolios

Manage portfolios separately and use the Grand Portfolio view where applicable.

📊 Reports & Analysis

📊 Portfolio Performance

Review portfolio-level performance and analytical summaries.

📋 Share Ledger

Inspect share-level ledger and analysis information for selected stocks.

🧾 Statement

Use the statement section to review portfolio-related records and summaries.

💰 Dividends

Maintain dividend records and review your dividend ledger.

📈 Value History

Explore portfolio value history and growth charts over time.

📅 Record Date

Check record-date related information available in the application.

🔎 Research & Market Tools

💡 Buy/Sell Signals

Review generated buy and sell signal information to support market research.

🔎 Stock Screener

Use screening tools such as Trend (PSAR), RSI and the Combined Scanner.

⭐ Market Watch

Follow selected market information and stock-related views from the research area.

🧠 Smart Signals

Explore the advanced signal workflow available to eligible users.

🔬 Deep Analysis

Use deeper analytical views for supported stock and market data.

📈 Advanced Charts

Access the advanced charting area available as a Pro feature.

🔐 Getting started

  1. From the public market page, select Login / Enter App.
  2. Sign in with your StockPulse account, or create an account if registration is enabled.
  3. Start with the Dashboard, then add/manage your portfolio and explore the research tools.
  4. Use the available reports, charts and analysis sections according to your workflow.
