About the Creator

Muhammad Mahady Hasan

Data, statistics and market analysis are at the heart of StockPulse — a practical platform built to make Bangladesh stock-market information easier to explore.

👋 A little about me

I’m Muhammad Mahady Hasan. I completed my Master’s in Statistics and Data Science and have professional experience working as a Data Scientist.

My work and interests include data analysis, large datasets, statistical thinking and extracting useful insights from complex data. I have also worked with stock-market data and big-data style analysis, which inspired me to build StockPulse as a focused tool for market and portfolio analysis.

📊 Why StockPulse?

StockPulse is designed around a simple idea: bring useful market information and analysis tools together in one place, instead of making investors jump between different sources.

🇧🇩 Bangladesh-focused

Built around DSE market data, portfolio tracking and Bangladesh-market workflows.

🔁 DSE + CSE comparison

Where supporting data is available, StockPulse can bring DSE and CSE information together so users can compare the two markets more easily.

📈 Data-driven analysis

Market data, historical records, indicators and analytical views are combined to support deeper research.

🎯 Built for practical use

The goal is not just to display numbers, but to turn market data into a cleaner and more useful workflow.

🚀 The idea behind the project

StockPulse is an independent project that I’m continuously improving. The focus is on reliable data workflows, useful analysis, a clean interface and features that can help users understand their portfolio and the broader market more clearly.

The platform will continue to evolve as new data sources, analytical methods and user needs are added.
